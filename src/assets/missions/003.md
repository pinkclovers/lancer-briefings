# Mission // #003

## SOME SICK GAME

*Gone ‘til the rain stops.*

– Outlander euphemism for “dead”

# Goals

- Travel to Furnace City. Navigate the wilderness of Asphodel. Rescue abducted Calliopeans. Wreck the arena. Send Rodericke Steele a clear message: the lives of Calliope’s people are not his playthings.

# Stakes

- A lot of people will be forced to kill each other for sport if they aren’t rescued, and Steele will be further emboldened to exploit Calliope.

# Reserves

- Mistress Elske will provide the team with enough funds to advance to LICENSE LEVEL 3. She’s also promised to upgrade the Dragon’s Tooth.

# Rewards
- You have access to any reserves you've acquired from the last two missions or on downtime activities along with you, so long as it makes narrative sense for them to be available on Asphodel.
