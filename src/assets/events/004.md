Right, kinda forgot that we did these, not sure if the dates are right but a few quick notes from what I got back from Rita I suppose.

**THE MISSING SCIENTIST**
Harrison Armory's scientist was missing, **Dr. Nyambose**, laser physicist that according to Loki Valentinian "made lasers so powerful they cut holes in space." Was being held captive by the Cult, they needed her help to construct a beam capable of cracking Cantele.

**THE UNAUTHORIZED REPAIRS**
The local union was on strike after Rita was framed for authorizng the **Faith of the One's** repairs on the Icebreaker's generators. Upon questioning with the faith, they provided the work order without question and their story was that the credentials were validated and that it was not forged by them nor was it their intention to cause a labor dispute.

The overseer of the operation, **Dr. Rose Bottlé** a engineer and reactor specialist arrived on the station without warning and left just as silently once the repairs were done, when asking members of the Faith about her, some were surprised saying "I thought she was in Djall" a system light years away.

Further investigation however lead back to warehouse owned by the Faith, specifically in the name of Dr. Rose which had been having more activity at that time.

**THE STOLEN BLUEPRINT**
The team received a call from **Riley Hyeon**, an Icebreaker civic achivist, informing them the office had an audit and discovered the servers were accessed without authorization, copying old blueprints for the Borealis. The older versions of the blueprints included blueprints for the mining laser.

**THE PRINTER SNAFU**
There were many junk requests at the printers on the Icebreaker, SRT did not investigate but the problem was resolved soon after the team arrived at Deepest Fairyland.

**EVELINA BONDARCHUK**
Evelina Bondarchuk attempted strongarming the SRT to halt their investigation after ties to her involvement were found at the Gift Bronze Casino, upon the revelation of the Faith's plans Evelina fled the station and has been radio silent since.
