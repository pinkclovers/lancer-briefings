**THE MURDERED CENSUS-TAKER**
Tarben Makeli was murdered in his home by members of the **Cult of the One Plumed in Golden Flame** whom he trusted within his apartment, after investigating the recent appearances of undocumented individuals onto the Icebreaker from the Zheng Yi Sao district. His notes indicated that he suspected it was part of a trafficking operation.

**THE TRASHED DATACENTER**
The datacenter holding an off-site copy of Tarben Makali's notes was destroyed in the middle of a weekly backup by an unknown group that fled into the freight tunnels after the attack along with a lot of civic management data.

**UNDER THE ZHENG YI SAO DISTRICT**
The source of the wiggle, constant alarms, and power issues within the district originated from within the freight tunnels. The hideout of the members of **The One** housed some sort of blink-style gate emitting glowing flames from a bright, lightbending, hole called a "Fire Gate".
